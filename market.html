The Gap We Fill

Artisanal miners capture only 40–60% of gold via gravity. The remaining 40–60% stays in tailings as fine particles too small for sluices and shaking tables. Legion Mining buys these tailings, leaches the fine gold via CIL, and sells refined doré — turning waste into revenue for both miners and investors.

Validated Thesis

The "no milling" claim is not theoretical. Artisanal miners in Kakamega already use ball mills as part of their gravity recovery process. Legion buys the waste tailings they discard. This is a tailings reprocessing model, not a greenfield mining operation.
