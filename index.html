The Story

Miller Shikoli is a 21-year-old entrepreneur from Kakamega County, Western Kenya — a region he knows not just as a hometown, but as a goldfield with enormous untapped potential. Born and raised in Kakamega, Miller spent formative years in Mombasa and Nairobi before returning home with a clear vision: to build a structured, profitable gold ore processing operation at the grassroots level.

The Mission

What drives Miller isn't just the business opportunity — it's the gap he saw between artisanal miners who have the ore and the market that pays for refined gold. Legion Mining Company is his answer to that gap: a reliable buyer, a professional processor, and a business that creates real value in the local economy.

Without a formal business school background, Miller has built his knowledge from the field — understanding ore grades, supplier dynamics, CIL processing chemistry, and investor relations through hands-on research and relentless execution.

How It Works

From Ore to Refined Gold

Our four-stage pipeline ensures maximum extraction yield and consistent quality output at every batch cycle.

01
Ore Sourcing

We purchase raw gold ore directly from verified artisanal miners across Kakamega County at competitive field prices.

02
Crushing & Milling

Ore is crushed and milled to a fine slurry to liberate gold particles and prepare them for leaching.

03
CIL Leaching

Carbon-in-leach processing dissolves gold using cyanide solution and adsorbs it onto activated carbon simultaneously.

04
Elution & Smelting

Gold is stripped from carbon, electrowon, and smelted into doré bars ready for refinery or direct sale.

Our Region

Why Kakamega County

Western Kenya is home to some of East Africa's richest artisanal gold deposits — and we operate at the heart of it.

Rich Mineral Belt

Kakamega County sits on the Nzoia gold belt — a historically productive artisanal gold mining zone with active deposits across multiple wards.

👷
Active Miner Base

Thousands of artisanal and small-scale miners operate in our region, providing a reliable and growing ore supply chain for our processing model.
