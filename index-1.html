Who We Are

Carbon-in-Leach Processing in the Heart of Kenya's Gold Belt

Legion Mining Company is a gold ore purchasing and CIL processing operation based in Kakamega County — one of Kenya's most productive artisanal mining regions within the Lake Victoria Greenstone Belt.

We buy raw ore from small-scale miners, process it through Carbon-in-Leach technology, and recover 85–92% of actual gold content — compared to 40–60% from traditional mercury amalgamation.

Founded and operated by Miller Shikoli, every aspect of this business is built on field documentation, lab verification, and hands-on operational experience — not projections alone.

The host rock at our primary site is Syenite — a credible and well-documented gold-bearing formation in the East African geological record.
